# RETAIL SALES, ITEM & OUTLET PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
The project helps the recommendations to optimize shelf placement, inventory stock levels, and store performance
# Domain :
Retail
# Dataset Information:
Source: Hugging Face / BigMart-Sales Details Dataset / ⁠ https://huggingface.co/datasets/sayanroy058/BigMart-Sales-Data
Year / Timeline: Data collected during 1985 to 2009
# Dataset Column/features Description:
Item_Identifier – Unique ID assigned to each product.
Item_Weight – Weight of the product.
Item_Fat_Content – Indicates whether the product is low fat or regular.
Item_Visibility – Percentage of total display area allocated to the item in the store shelf..
Item_Type – Category of the product.
Item_MRP – Maximum Retail Price of the product..
Outlet_Identifier – Unique ID assigned to each store location.
Outlet_Establishment_Year – Year the specific store was opened.
Outlet_Size – Ground area size of the store.
Outlet_Location_Type – City tier rating of the store's location.
Outlet_Type – Classification of store format.
Item_Outlet_Sales – Total sales revenue generated by the item at that particular outlet.
# Overall Insights:
## 1. Descriptive Analysis:
Total Revenue: Your stores generated 18.59M Crore in total sales across 10 outlets and 1,559 products.
Top Best-Sellers: Fruits & Vegetables (2.8 M) and Snack Foods (2.7 M) brought in the highest sales.
Customer Choice: Low Fat products made up 64% of total sales compared to Regular products 36%.
## 2. Diagnostic Analysis:
Store Format: Supermarket Type 3 made the most money per order because bigger supermarkets offer more variety and higher-priced items.
Store Size: Medium-sized stores performed better than large (High-sized) stores, showing that bigger space doesn't always mean higher sales.
Small Stores Struggled: Small Grocery Stores had very low sales because they carry fewer products and have limited space.
## 3. Predictive Analysis:
Healthy Product Demand: Sales for Low Fat items will continue to grow as customers focus more on health.
Medium Store Success: Opening Medium-sized supermarkets will consistently bring in higher sales than opening small grocery shops.
Popular Items: High demand for Fruits, Vegetables, and Snack Foods will remain steady throughout the year.
## 4. Prescriptive Analysis:
Stock Top Products: Keep high inventory for Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods so you never run out of stock.
Focus on Medium Supermarkets: Expand by opening more Medium-sized Supermarkets instead of small grocery stores.
Promote Low Fat Items: Give better shelf display to Low Fat items to boost revenue further.
Fix Small Stores: Add special discounts or popular everyday items to small grocery stores to increase their sales.

# Conclusion
The dashboard shows that product price (Item MRP) and store type (Outlet Type) have the biggest effect on overall sales.
Placing items where customers can easily see them clearly helps boost total sales in every store.
Using sales predictions helps store managers pick the right items to stock instead of relying on pure guesswork.
Showing both actual past sales and expected future sales in one place gives leaders a quick, clear view of business performance.
This project creates a solid foundation that can easily expand later to include live stock tracking and automatic updates.
